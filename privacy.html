Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Effective date: November 24, 2026

1. Who we are

FollowDeck ("the app") is a follow-up and client-tracking tool for independent service providers. The data controller is the developer account listed on the App Store product page. Contact: 463102527@qq.com.

2. What data the app processes

The first build is local-first: no account, no cross-device sync, no analytics or advertising SDK. Feedback leaves only through the system mail composer as a draft; the app does not upload feedback automatically. All client, deal, follow-up and interaction records stay on the device.

The app processes information you choose to enter:

  • Client records: name, phone, email, tags and notes you enter.
  • Deal records: title, stage, amount, currency, next action, due date, internal note.
  • Follow-ups and interactions: type, note, due date, channel, summary, date, optional duration.
  • Feedback: a message you compose in the system mail app, together with the app version and device model included in the draft. The app does not upload feedback on its own.

We do not collect your contacts' address books automatically; contact details are stored only when you add or import a specific record.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Local-first: client, deal, follow-up and interaction data is stored on your device by default.
  • Sync (Pro, planned): if you enable cross-device sync, your data is transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored encrypted on the sync backend. Sync can be disabled at any time.
  • Feedback: in the current build a feedback message is composed in the system mail app as a draft; the app itself does not upload it. If a future feedback channel transmits a message and optional screenshot, that happens only when you submit it, and is used to fix issues and plan features, not for advertising.

4. How we use your data

  • To schedule and display your reminders, follow-ups and pipeline.
  • To surface next-step suggestions and risk flags based on your own records.
  • To respond to feedback and improve the app.
  • To provide the Pro features you subscribe to, including receipt and subscription status.

We do not sell your personal data, do not use it for third-party advertising, and do not build advertising profiles from it.

5. AI features

The app uses AI in layers, ordered to keep your data on device wherever possible:

  • Local rule engine (shipped): next-step suggestions and risk flags are derived from your own records on device; no model call is made.
  • On-device model (planned, system-native): where the device supports it, draft suggestions may use the Apple on-device Foundation Model through the system framework. This processing stays on device; older or unsupported systems fall back to the local rule engine.
  • Private Cloud Compute (reserved): a more complex task may later use Apple's Private Cloud Compute under Apple's privacy framework. If enabled, the transmitted categories and purposes are declared in the Privacy Nutrition Label before that build ships.
  • Third-party or self-hosted model (reserved, off by default): only if you enable such a feature is the minimal necessary context sent to the chosen provider; the provider's retention and training terms are linked here once finalized, and the data category is declared in the label.

AI output is a suggestion only. Amounts, stages and commitments remain based on the data you enter; the app does not invent client details or payments. You review and edit any draft before acting on it.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Service providers who operate the sync backend, payment processing or hosting, under contracts that restrict use to providing the service;
  • When required by law or valid legal process.

Third-party SDKs included in the build are listed in the app's Privacy Manifest and in the App Store Privacy Nutrition Label. The current build targets no advertising or analytics SDK.

7. Your rights and choices

In the app you can:

  • Export your client, deal and follow-up data from Settings.
  • Delete individual records or erase all app data from Settings.
  • Disable sync (once available) and keep data only on the device.
  • Withhold permissions (for example notifications); core records still work locally.

Depending on your region, you may also have statutory rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, port your data, withdraw consent, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights (CCPA/CPRA).

Under the GDPR / UK GDPR you may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority; under the CCPA/CPRA you may contact the relevant California privacy authority. Contact us to exercise these rights; we respond within the period required by applicable law. Because the app stores data locally, most access, correction and deletion is performed directly in the app.

8. Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or the app is removed. If a future feedback channel receives submitted feedback, it is retained for the period needed to support the relevant issue or feature. Backups and sync copies follow the same deletion requests.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age specified by applicable law, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. The expected age rating is 4+.

10. Security

Data in transit uses encryption; stored sync data is encrypted. Access to backend systems is restricted. No security measure is absolute; report suspected issues to the support contact.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at launch and for major features. Material changes will be noted in the app and on this page, with the updated effective date.
