Frequently asked questions

Is my client data stored on a server?

By default all client data stays on your device; the first build does not transmit it to a server. Cross-device sync is a planned future Pro feature; when it ships, data will be transmitted and stored encrypted and can be disabled.

Does FollowDeck send invoices or take payments?

No. FollowDeck is the follow-up layer around your existing invoicing and payment tools; it does not generate invoices, process payments, book meetings or do accounting.

How does the next-step suggestion work?

The first build derives suggestions from your own records on device using a local rule engine; your client data is not sent to a server for this. Planned system-native model assistance runs on device where supported, falling back to the rule engine on older systems. Every suggestion stays editable, and you review any draft before acting on it.

Does it work with Shortcuts, App Intents or widgets?

Yes. FollowDeck exposes App Intents for logging interactions, adding follow-ups, checking what is next, completing follow-ups, looking up a client status and getting a next-step suggestion. It includes home-screen widgets, Shortcuts actions and Spotlight search.

How do I back up or delete my data?

Use Settings → Export to save a local JSON or CSV copy. You can delete individual records or erase all app data from Settings. Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app, subject to your device backup settings.

How do subscriptions, refunds or cancellations work?

Pro is billed through the Apple App Store in-app purchase system. The price, plan length and any trial terms are shown before purchase. Renewal, billing, refunds and cancellation are managed in your Apple Account settings; the developer does not control Apple's billing or refund policies.
